Date: 2026-11-14. Spec: docs/specs/v3.md (V3-1 to V3-7). Instruction: the owner asked for v3 of the specs and continued building to a final polished product.

## How v3 was chosen

Screenshots of the v2 build were reviewed: `node .agents/shots.mjs <folder>` (the folder was in the session's temporary scratchpad, not committed). Findings: no single place showed what waits for the advocate, inferred dates looked like explicit dates, dates were shown as ISO, the chat dock was empty with no guidance, some buttons stretched across the view, the source table scrolled sideways, and the landing page still said "Attach PDF files". There was no way to run the application without the development tools.

## Mismatches and decisions recorded

- The v3 spec first said the mailbox moves from the Filing tab to Settings. As built, the same form is on both, because the Filing tab also imports from the mailbox. The spec was corrected.
- The build ships without an ASAR archive. The Euro-Office converter and the validator are executables, and the application finds its resources beside its code. electron-builder warns about this. Packing with ASAR and unpacking the executables is parked.
- The application version is now 1.0.0.

## Not verified

- No owner proof task on a real brief (lesson L3), for v1, v2 or v3.
- The build is not code-signed, and there is no installer program: the build is a folder. Only the Windows build was made and tested.
- The progress stages and their next actions are not tuned on a real matter.
- The form text of the petition and of the condonation application is a generic template, not checked against the Supreme Court Rules.
